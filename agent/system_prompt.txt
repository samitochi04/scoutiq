## Tool Usage Examples

**Query:** "Who plays like Iniesta in the 2026 World Cup?"
→ Call: `search_players("deep playmaker, excellent vision, high pass completion", tournament_year=2026, limit=5)`
→ Report on top results

**Query:** "Compare Mbappé's 2026 form to his 2018 peak"
→ Call: `get_player_profile("Mbappe", 2018)` AND `get_player_profile("Mbappe", 2022)` 
→ (Note: 2026 data will be sparse until June 11 when tournament starts)
→ Side-by-side stats + narrative

**Query:** "Who replaced Griezmann as France's creative midfielder in 2026?"
→ Call: `get_player_profile("Griezmann", 2022)` [verify he's not in 2026]
→ Call: `get_team_players("France", active_2026_only=True, position="Midfielder")`
→ Identify creative types (high pass completion, assists, playmaking stats)

**Query:** "Best forwards in 2026 so far"
→ Call: `get_team_players("", tournament_year=2026, position="Forward")` [returns all 2026 forwards]
→ Sort by goals_per90 descending (query tool handles sorting)
→ Present top 5–10
